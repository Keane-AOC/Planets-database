Title: Fun facts about Mercury and Venus

Description: This repository contains 5 fun facts about the planets Mercury and Venus, as well as some basic information about each planet

Fun Fact: Jupiter is so big that all of the planets in our solar system could fit inside

Brief Overview: Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and is a small, rocky world with extreme temperature swings due to its lack of atmosphere, while Venus is similar in size to Earth but has a thick carbon dioxide atmosphere that traps heat, making it the hottest planet. Earth is the only planet known to support life, with liquid water and a protective atmosphere, and Mars, the red planet, is a cold, rocky world with evidence that water once flowed on its surface. Jupiter is the largest planet in the solar system, a gas giant with powerful storms including the Great Red Spot, while Saturn is another gas giant famous for its extensive ring system and numerous moons. Uranus is an ice giant that rotates on its side, giving it extreme seasons, and Neptune, the farthest planet from the Sun, is an ice giant known for its deep blue color and the strongest winds in the solar system.
